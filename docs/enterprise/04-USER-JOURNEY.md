# Enterprise Platform

# 04 User Journey

## Purpose

This document defines how different personas achieve their business goals through AI Fitness Solution.

User journeys describe user success, not internal technical workflows.

The platform should guide users from business needs to successful outcomes.

---

# Journey 1

# Enterprise Customer Journey

## Persona

Enterprise Customer

## Product Line

Enterprise Builder

## Goal

Build an enterprise fitness space.

---

## User Journey

### Step 1

## Discover

User enters AI Fitness Solution.

User selects:

"Build Enterprise Fitness Space"

---

### Step 2

## Understand Need

AI Assistant collects:

- Company size
- Employee number
- Location
- Space information
- Budget expectation
- Business goals

---

### Step 3

## AI Planning

AI analyzes:

- Space requirements
- Equipment recommendations
- Usage scenarios
- Investment range

---

### Step 4

## Review Solution

User reviews:

- Fitness Space Plan
- Recommended Configuration
- Budget Estimate

---

### Step 5

## Decision

User downloads:

- Solution Proposal
- Budget Report

---

## Success Definition

Enterprise customer obtains a professional fitness construction plan for internal decision making.
