# Business Principles

Business value always comes before product implementation.

Products support business.

AI supports products.

Technology supports AI.

---

# AI Native Principle

AI is not a feature.

AI is the operating layer of the platform.

The assistant guides users.

Users confirm decisions.

The platform executes automatically.

---

# Success Metric

A customer should be able to move from

Demand

↓

Growth

without understanding any internal implementation concepts.

Users should never need to understand:

- Quote Engine
- Budget Engine
- Tender Engine
- Workspace

The platform converts business goals into AI-driven workflows automatically.
