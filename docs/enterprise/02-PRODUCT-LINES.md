# Enterprise Platform

# 02 Product Lines

---

## Purpose

This document defines the major products of AI Fitness Solution.

Product Lines represent customer-facing business products.

Internal technical modules should not be exposed as products.

---

# Product Line 1

## Enterprise Builder

Target Users

Enterprise Customers

Goal

Help organizations plan and build fitness spaces.

Business Value

Demand → Planning → Proposal

Outputs

- Solution Plan
- Budget
- Project Proposal

---

# Product Principles

One user goal maps to one product line.

Products expose business value.

Modules support products.

Technical engines remain internal.
