# V19.1 Brand & Catalog Intelligence Report

**Version:** `v19.1-brand-catalog-intelligence-1`  
**Predecessor:** V19 Bidder Intelligence Foundation

## Executive Summary

Brand & Catalog Intelligence 将 V19 框架升级为可查询的品牌与设备知识资产层。

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Brand Coverage | 品牌库覆盖度（目标 7 品牌，三级定位） |
| Equipment Coverage | 设备库覆盖度（目标 10 型号，5 分类） |
| Budget Coverage | 低/中/高预算映射完整度 |
| Differentiation Readiness | 品牌比较 + 设备匹配综合差异化就绪度 |

## Catalog Completeness

`runCatalogCoverageRuntime()` 统计：

- brand coverage (%)
- category coverage (%)
- equipment coverage (%)
- **Catalog Completeness Score** (三者均值)

## Dashboard Readiness

`runBrandCatalogDashboardRuntime()` 输出：

- brand readiness
- equipment readiness
- comparison readiness
- matching readiness
- budget mapping readiness
