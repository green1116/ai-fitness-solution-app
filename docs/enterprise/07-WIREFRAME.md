# Enterprise Platform

# 07 Wireframe

## Purpose

This document defines the structural layout of major product pages.

Wireframes describe page structure and user interaction flow.

They do not define visual styling.

# Wireframe Principles

## Principle 1

Every page has one primary user goal.

---

## Principle 2

AI should appear as a guide, not an extra feature.

---

## Principle 3

Users should not navigate technical modules.

---

## Principle 4

Complexity appears only when needed.

---

## Principle 5

First-time experience and returning experience are different.
